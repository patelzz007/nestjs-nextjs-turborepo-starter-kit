# Surface brief — mobile app (whole app)

Scope: every screen of apps/mobile and the packages/ui-native kit it renders with. Mode: Operate.
Audience: teams starting products from the kit, and the people those products serve. Job: sign in,
read account and activity at a glance, change settings, review. Constraint: the bottom floating tab
bar stays exactly as it was (geometry, colours, label face); functionality is unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, executed to Linear/Things precision with Airbnb/Notion roominess: a
calm, generic account app any product can rebrand. It refuses boxes-in-boxes: no curved sheet under
the header, no bordered card around every block, no centred small titles.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral ink, zero tint. Light: soft grey page, near-white surfaces with hairline edges,
near-black ink. Dark: pure greys by elevation, near-white ink. Nord: Polar Night surfaces, Snow Storm
ink. Colour does jobs only: blue for links and focus, toned status, chart series. One face, Geist, in a
real ramp (34 title, 20 section, 17 row, 15 secondary, 13 caption) with tabular figures.

STORY: Users see where they are (large left title), what matters (the first number or the account),
and what they can do (grouped, iconed rows), in that order, on every screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: menu, quiet status, bell on one pinned row; a 30pt left-aligned title with its
subtitle; an account row on a surface; Discover cards; tab bar unchanged at the bottom.

FORM: canon (the category standard), chosen by the user over the roll. seed:e16ec4cc6cf14245

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
