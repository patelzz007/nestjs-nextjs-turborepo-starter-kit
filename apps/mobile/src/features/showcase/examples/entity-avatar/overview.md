## When to use

- Shops, tenants and teams, with or without a logo

## When not to use

- People: see UserAvatar

## Notes

- On a phone it is the kit's `Avatar` with `shape="rounded"`: a logo is contained on a plain tile, never cropped
- `tone="name"` tints the monogram from the name (shared with the web)

## EntityAvatar

An organisation's logo, or a monogram tinted from its name: the kit's `Avatar`, rounded.

### Features

- **Logo** contained on a tile.
- **Name-tinted monogram**: the same name, the same colour.

## Integrating with other components

Use it for workspace switchers, merchant lists and team pickers.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will a shop keep its colour?

Yes: the tint is worked out from the name, the same on the web and the phone.
