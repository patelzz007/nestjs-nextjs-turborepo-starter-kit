## When to use

- A few photos of one thing: a product, a listing, a place

## When not to use

- Mixed slides with text and actions: use Carousel

## Notes

- Built on the kit's Carousel: swipe, arrows and a counter
- Controlled: the caller holds the shown index

## ImageCarousel

`ImageCarousel` is a ready-made photo gallery: swipe between photos, with arrows and a counter.

### Features

- **Counter** (2 of 5) and **arrows**.
- **Ratios** of the frame.

## Integrating with other components

Put it at the top of a product or listing screen; pair it with thumbnails for long galleries (see Carousel).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know which photo is shown?

It is controlled: `index` and `onChange` keep it in your state.
