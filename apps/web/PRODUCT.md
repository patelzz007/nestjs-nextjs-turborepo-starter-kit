# Product — Web app

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Inherits the root `PRODUCT.md`; only what differs is recorded here.

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Customers** of RewardHub merchants, often on a phone.
- **Visitors** reaching the public landing page.

## Product Purpose

- **The authenticated dashboard:** browse rewards, claim them, show the claim's QR code (with
  a backup code) at the counter, follow referrals, and see one's own spending.
- **The public landing page.**

## Operating Context

- **At the counter:** a claim is redeemed at the till, so the QR screen must be quick to reach
  and easy to scan.
- **Email verification:** seeded customers start unverified, which demonstrates the
  verify-email flow.

## Brand Commitments

The web accent is blue.

## Evidence on Hand

No real customer testimonials, usage figures or merchant logos exist. The landing page must
not invent them.
